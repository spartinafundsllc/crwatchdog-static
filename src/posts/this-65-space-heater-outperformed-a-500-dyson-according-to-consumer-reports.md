When most people think of “the best” space heater, a high-end Dyson usually comes to mind.
The **Dyson Purifier Hot+Cool HP1** is sleek, smart, and often priced around **$500**. But in Consumer Reports testing, a much cheaper model quietly pulled ahead: the **Lasko Artisan Series CX16550**, which typically sells for about **$61–$65**.

Both heaters are **Consumer Reports Recommended** and labeled a **Green Choice**, but the Lasko actually earned a slightly higher overall score: **84 vs. Dyson’s 83**. If your main goal is reliable, efficient heat for winter, the budget heater may be the smarter buy.

## [Lasko Artisan Series CX16550: Budget Heater, Top-Tier Performance](https://geni.us/Kh86b)

The **Lasko Artisan Series CX16550** is a compact ceramic space heater that focuses on the basics: fast heat, good safety features, and solid build quality.

*   **Consumer Reports Overall Score:** 84
*   **Approximate Price:** $61–$65
*   **Type:** Ceramic space heater
*   **Maximum Output:** 1500 watts
*   **Size:** ~15.5 x 8.4 x 8.4 inches
*   **Weight:** ~5.1 pounds
*   **Safety Features:** Tip-over switch, overheat protection
*   **Portability:** No built-in handle; designed for floor or desk use
*   **Warranty:** 3 years

In Consumer Reports testing, the Lasko delivers strong, consistent heating performance at its 1500-watt output. It’s light, easy to move between rooms, and includes key safety features like a tip-over switch and overheat protection. It doesn’t have app control, air purification, or voice commands—but that’s exactly why it’s such a standout value: almost all of its cost goes into doing one thing well: [**heating your space**](https://geni.us/Kh86b).

## Dyson Purifier Hot+Cool HP1: [Premium Design, Premium Price](https://geni.us/4cKw)

The **Dyson Purifier Hot+Cool HP1** is much more than a simple space heater. It’s a tall, bladeless unit that functions as a **convection heater, fan, and air purifier** in one.

*   **Consumer Reports Overall Score:** 83
*   **Approximate Price:** ~$499.95
*   **Type:** Convection heater + fan + purifier
*   **Maximum Output:** 1500 watts
*   **Size:** ~30.0 x 9.8 x 9.8 inches
*   **Weight:** ~11.7 pounds
*   **Safety Features:** Tip-over switch, overheat protection
*   **Smart Features:** App control, voice control, scheduling via myDyson app
*   **Warranty:** 3 years

The Dyson excels if you want an **all-in-one climate and air quality solution**. You can monitor and control it via the myDyson app, set schedules, and use voice commands. It looks premium and feels high-tech. But when you strip away the extras and look purely at **heating performance and value**, the Dyson doesn’t meaningfully beat the Lasko in Consumer Reports’s scoring—despite costing roughly **seven to eight times more**.

## Which Space Heater Should You Buy?

### Choose the [Lasko Artisan Series](https://geni.us/Kh86b) CX16550 if:

*   You primarily want an **affordable, reliable space heater** for winter.
*   You care more about **performance and safety** than smart features.
*   You like the idea of a heater that **scored slightly higher** than a premium Dyson in Consumer Reports testing.
*   You’re heating a **single room or office** and don’t need built-in air purification.

### Choose the [Dyson](https://geni.us/4cKw) Purifier Hot+Cool HP1 if:

*   You want an **all-in-one device** that heats, cools, and purifies the air.
*   You value **smart-home integration**, app control, and scheduling.
*   You’re willing to pay a premium for **design and multi-function convenience**.

For most people shopping specifically for a **space heater**, the conclusion is simple: **the Lasko Artisan Series CX16550 offers better heating value than the Dyson HP1**. The Dyson is still a great product—but if you don’t need air purification and smart controls, Consumer Reports’s scores suggest you can save hundreds of dollars without giving up performance.

## Key Takeaway

If your priority is **staying warm for less money**, the **$65 Lasko Artisan Series CX16550** is the smarter, more efficient choice. It slightly outperforms the **$500 Dyson Purifier Hot+Cool HP1** in Consumer Reports’s overall score, matches it on power and core safety features, and costs a fraction of the price.
