## What Does DeleteMe Actually Do?

Removing your private information from data brokers manually is like playing an endless game of whack-a-mole. While you have the legal right to submit opt-out requests under state privacy laws, each broker requires a separate form, email verification, or even scanned IDs—and they routinely re-add your profile a few months later when new public records are scraped.

**DeleteMe solves this by automating the entire removal lifecycle:**

1. **Comprehensive Scanning:** DeleteMe's privacy operators continuously search over **750+ data broker directories** and people-search search engines for your name, phone numbers, home addresses, email aliases, and family connections.
2. **Hands-Off Removal Requests:** Dedicated privacy experts submit formal opt-out requests directly to data broker databases on your behalf, navigating their complex bureaucratic opt-out loopholes.
3. **Continuous Monitoring:** Because brokers frequently re-publish records after state voter rolls or real estate transactions are updated, DeleteMe scans for your info every 90 days.
4. **Detailed Privacy Reports:** Every quarter, you receive an in-depth progress report showing exactly which broker sites held your data, which records were successfully scrubbed, and what is currently being processed.
