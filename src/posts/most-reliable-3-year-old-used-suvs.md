A 3-year-old used SUV sits right in the automotive sweet spot. Because new vehicles suffer their steepest depreciation cliff within the first 36 months, buying a model from the 2023 model year allows you to secure modern styling, contemporary powertrains, and cutting-edge active safety tech at a massive discount compared to showroom sticker prices. (If you prefer an older, even more budget-friendly bracket, see our guide to the [Most Reliable 5-Year-Old Used SUVs](/most-reliable-5-year-old-used-suvs/).)

To determine which 2023 models hold up over the long haul, [Consumer Reports](https://www.consumerreports.org/) analyzed real-world survey data covering roughly **380,000 vehicles**.

Every SUV listed below earned top marks for proven reliability, backed by real-world owner experiences and comprehensive track testing.

## Part 1: Top Reliable Two-Row SUVs

### 1. [Toyota Venza (2023)](https://www.toyota.com/venza/)

* **Price:** $27,750 – $32,950
* **Powertrain:** 2.5-liter 4-Cylinder Hybrid (219 hp combined) | Standard AWD
* **The Blueprint:** Utilizing the proven RAV4 Hybrid architecture, the Venza delivers exceptional fuel efficiency (39 owner-reported mpg / 37 mpg CR test). It delivers a comfortable ride and secure handling. High-end Limited trims offer an electrochromic dimmable glass roof.
* **The Catch:** Pronounced engine and tire noise during hard acceleration, alongside a touch-heavy control interface.

### 2. [Toyota 4Runner (2023)](https://www.toyota.com/4runner/)

* **Price:** $33,400 – $50,650
* **Powertrain:** 4.0-liter V6 | Part-Time 4WD with Low Range
* **The Blueprint:** Built with old-school body-on-frame construction, generous ground clearance, and standard underbody skid plates, the 4Runner is nearly indestructible on trails. Big physical dash knobs make control inputs simple.
* **The Catch:** Clumsy handling, significant body lean in corners, a firm ride, and a thirsty 18 mpg overall.
* **Safety Note:** Forward Collision Warning (FCW) and Automatic Emergency Braking (AEB) are standard; Blind Spot Warning (BSW) and Rear Cross Traffic Warning (RCTW) require SR5 Premium, TRD Off-Road Premium, Limited, or TRD PRO trims.

### 3. [Cadillac XT5 (2023)](https://www.cadillac.com/suvs/xt5)

* **Price:** $23,700 – $32,700
* **Powertrain:** 2.0-liter Turbo 4-Cylinder or 3.6-liter V6
* **The Blueprint:** A solid, planted chassis that resists body lean through turns. The front seats are exceptionally supportive, and the base 2.0-liter turbo feels punchy and responsive in everyday traffic.
* **The Catch:** The 3.6L V6 feels slightly lethargic despite linear power delivery. BSW and RCTW are standard on all trims except the base model.

### 4. [Nissan Murano (2023)](https://www.nissanusa.com/vehicles/crossovers-suvs/murano.html)

* **Price:** $19,100 – $25,850
* **Powertrain:** 3.5-liter V6 with CVT
* **The Blueprint:** A quiet, upscale cabin with plush seating at the lowest entry price in the category. The 3.5-liter V6 delivers refined acceleration.
* **The Catch:** Light, uninspiring steering and a CVT tuned purely for relaxed cruising rather than performance. Stick to 18-inch wheels for the best ride quality.

### 5. [Ford Edge (2023)](https://www.ford.com/suvs/edge/)

* **Price:** $20,450 – $27,950
* **Powertrain:** 2.0-liter Turbo 4-Cylinder with 8-Speed Automatic
* **The Blueprint:** Quick 0–60 mph acceleration (7.7 seconds) with zero turbo lag, confidence-inspiring handling, low step-in height, and generous door openings.
* **The Catch:** Fuel economy (22 mpg test) is mediocre for a 4-cylinder crossover.

### 6. [Subaru Outback (2023)](https://www.subaru.com/vehicles/outback.html)

* **Price:** $21,350 – $27,525
* **Powertrain:** 2.5-liter 4-Cylinder (28 CR mpg) or 260-hp 2.4-liter Turbo (24 CR mpg)
* **The Blueprint:** The benchmark for wagon-style versatility. Standard AWD, built-in roof crossbars, a dual-position cargo cover, and exceptional suspension compliance over rough terrain.
* **The Catch:** Base 2.5L engine feels strained under heavy passenger loads; infotainment requires navigating multi-layer menus. BSW and RCTW were optional.

## Part 2: Top Reliable Three-Row SUVs

### 7. [Buick Enclave (2023)](https://www.buick.com/suvs/enclave)

* **Price:** $25,725 – $33,025
* **Powertrain:** 3.6-liter V6 (310 hp) with 9-Speed Automatic | 5,000-lb Towing
* **The Blueprint:** Flagship acoustic insulation creates a serene cabin environment. The third row provides genuine legroom for adults—a rarity in this segment.
* **The Catch:** Push-button electronic shifter is awkward during parking maneuvers; test fuel economy clocked in at 18 mpg.

### 8. [Lexus GX (2023)](https://www.lexus.com/models/GX)

* **Price:** $52,675 – $56,225
* **Powertrain:** 4.6-liter V8 with Full-Time 4WD
* **The Blueprint:** Silky-smooth V8 durability, outstanding off-road capability, physical dash controls, and a vault-like, whisper-quiet cabin. All key ADAS safety features came standard.
* **The Catch:** Heavy depreciation, steep fuel consumption (17 mpg), clumsy on-road handling, and a cramped third-row seat.

### 9. [Acura MDX (2023)](https://www.acura.com/suvs/mdx)

* **Price:** $31,550 – $42,975
* **Powertrain:** 290-hp 3.5-liter V6 with 10-Speed Automatic
* **The Blueprint:** Excellent luxury value relative to European rivals. Wide, supportive front seating and a removable center section in the second row that creates an easy pass-through aisle to the third row.
* **The Catch:** Infotainment operates via a finicky center-console touchpad rather than a direct touchscreen.

### 10. [Toyota Highlander & Highlander Hybrid (2023)](https://www.toyota.com/highlander/)

* **Price:** $28,200 – $41,900
* **Powertrain:** 295-hp 3.5-liter V6 or 2.5-liter 4-Cylinder Hybrid
* **The Blueprint:** The undisputed reliability benchmark for three-row haulers. The gas V6 provides ample highway passing reserve, while the Hybrid achieves an outstanding 35 mpg in CR lab testing.
* **The Catch:** The third row is strictly sized for small children.
* **Safety Note:** On gas models, BSW and RCTW were optional; on the Hybrid, both systems came standard on all trims above the base model.

---

## The Watchdog Verdict

* **For Maximum Fuel Efficiency:** The **Toyota Venza** (39 owner mpg) for 2-row buyers, and the **Toyota Highlander Hybrid** (34–35 mpg) for 3-row families.
* **For Under-$25k Value:** The **Nissan Murano** ($19,100+) and **Ford Edge** ($20,450+) offer high-end comfort and quiet highway cruising at accessible price points.
* **For True Heavy-Duty Durability:** The body-on-frame **Toyota 4Runner** and **Lexus GX** remain unmatched for mechanical longevity and rough-road capability.
