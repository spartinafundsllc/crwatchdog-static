If you are upgrading your kitchen and prefer the sleek look and easy cleanup of a glass smoothtop range, the market is completely flooded with options. But as we constantly remind our readers at CR Watchdog, a shiny exterior often hides a weak heating element or an oven that bakes completely unevenly. 

You need an appliance that can bring a giant pot of pasta water to a rapid boil *and* delicately melt chocolate without scorching it. 

Today, we are looking at the **[LG LREN6325XE](https://geni.us/KC8r)**, a 30-inch freestanding electric smoothtop range. Priced around $995, it sits right in the competitive mid-tier bracket. But does it deliver high-end performance? 

To find out, we pulled the rigorous, independent lab data directly from the highly respected Consumer Reports. The results are staggering. Earning the coveted status of a **"CR Recommended" pick** and an Overall Score of 79, this LG model absolutely dominated the performance tests. Here is our detailed, no-nonsense breakdown of why this range deserves a spot in your kitchen.

## Flawless Lab Performance: The Perfect 5/5 Sweep

When the experts at Consumer Reports test a range, they don't just turn it on and see if it gets hot. They run standardized, repeatable tests to measure exact performance. In an incredibly rare showing, the **[LG LREN6325XE](https://geni.us/KC8r)** earned a perfect 5/5 score across almost every single major testing category.

Here is how the data breaks down:

* **Cooktop High-Heat (5/5):** This test measures how quickly the most powerful element can bring a large pot of room-temperature water to a near boil. Thanks to its two high-power elements (pushing out 3200 watts and 3000 watts), the LG aced this test. If you frequently sear meats or cook stir-fry, this cooktop will give you the intense, sustained heat you need.
* **Cooktop Low-Heat (5/5):** Power is nothing without control. This test evaluates how well the lowest-powered element can hold a low temperature for melting chocolate, or how well a high-power element can hold a massive pot of tomato sauce below a simmer. The LG’s two 1200-watt low-power elements handled this flawlessly, meaning you won't be burning delicate sauces. 
* **Baking Evenness (5/5):** To test the oven, experts bake multiple racks of cookies and cakes simultaneously to look for hot and cold spots. The results from this LG oven were "hard to beat," delivering perfectly even browning across multiple racks. 
* **Broiling (5/5):** If you use your broiler to finish off burgers, steaks, or fish, you need intense, direct top-down heat. The LG earned an "Excellent" rating here, providing steakhouse-level searing capability. 
* **Usable Oven Capacity (5/5):** Manufacturers often exaggerate how much space is actually inside their ovens. While LG claims a 6.3-cubic-foot capacity, the lab verified that this model indeed offers a massive amount of highly usable space, making it perfect for hosting Thanksgiving or cooking multiple large dishes at once.

## Features That Actually Matter

Beyond the raw heating data, the **[LG LREN6325XE](https://geni.us/KC8r)** packs several practical features that make daily cooking and cleaning significantly easier:

* **Hidden Bake Element:** The bottom heating element is hidden beneath the floor of the oven. If a pie bubbles over or a casserole spills, you can simply wipe the flat bottom of the oven clean without having to awkwardly scrub around a fragile, exposed heating coil.
* **Multiple Cooking Modes:** The oven isn't just for standard baking. It features built-in settings for Convection, Air Fry, Slow Cook, Keep Warm, and Proofing (for bread makers). 
* **Self-Cleaning:** It features a high-temperature self-clean cycle that earned a very respectable 4/5 rating in the lab.
* **Smart Features:** It is Wi-Fi enabled, allowing you to monitor and control your oven remotely. It also utilizes a numeric keypad for quickly entering exact temperatures and cooking times.
* **Warming Element:** While it features 4 main cooking elements, the cooktop also includes a dedicated warming zone to keep side dishes hot while you finish the main course. 

## The Watchdog Reality Check: What's the Catch?

Usually, this is the part of the review where we tell you about the fatal flaw—the terrible control panel, the noisy fan, or the cheap plastic knobs. 

However, Consumer Reports' official verdict on this model is incredibly rare: **"This model had no discernible flaws in its performance."**

If we had to nitpick, we would point out the element configuration. The cooktop features two high-power elements and two low-power elements, but absolutely *zero* medium-power elements. You will have to rely on turning the high-power burners down to achieve a medium heat, which might require a slight learning curve for some home chefs. 

## Reliability and Owner Satisfaction

Performance in a lab is great, but will the control board fry itself in two years? 

Based on survey data from over 17,000 members who purchased electric smoothtop ranges, LG ranks #6 out of 13 brands for Predicted Reliability, earning a score of 67/100. This places it solidly in the upper-middle of the pack—meaning it is highly likely to give you years of trouble-free cooking.

When it comes to Owner Satisfaction, LG actually ranks #3 out of 13 brands. While the raw score is a 44/100, this is historically a tough category for electric ranges across the board. The #3 ranking indicates that LG owners are generally much happier with their purchases than buyers who went with competing brands.
