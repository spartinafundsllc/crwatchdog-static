No matter how much you spend on a dishwasher, it has one simple job: get your dishes clean and dry. Yet, if you have ever opened a dishwasher to find crusty, wet plates, you know that finding a machine that actually works is harder than it looks.

To separate the workhorses from the duds, [Consumer Reports](https://www.consumerreports.org/) put 120 dishwashers through a brutal 30-hour testing gauntlet. This involves baking a secret, starchy goop onto white ceramic plates, running them through a cycle without pre-rinsing, and analyzing the cleaning results with precision imaging software.

We dug through their 2026 lab data to bring you the absolute best standard-sized (24-inch) dishwashers on the market—and the overpriced flops you need to avoid.

---

## The Watchdog’s Top Picks: The Best Dishwashers

If you want squeaky-clean dishes, dry plastics, and whisper-quiet operation, these models dominated the lab tests.

### 1. The Premium Kings: [Bosch Benchmark & 800 Series](https://www.amazon.com/Stainless-Control-Pocket-Handle-Dishwasher/dp/B0FBY6WX38/)

* **Models:** Bosch Benchmark SHP9PCM5N (Score: 85, ~$2,497) | Bosch 800 Series SHX78CM5N (Score: 85, ~$1,935)
* **The Watchdog Take:** Bosch has completely cemented its reputation as the king of dishwashers. Both of these top-tier models earned an elite Overall Score of 85. They are incredibly quiet, incredibly energy-efficient, and feature a brand-new motorized wash arm that spins independently rather than relying on water pressure. Check availability for the [Bosch 800 Series SHX78CM5N](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Bosch+800+Series+SHX78CM5N+Dishwasher) as well.
* **The Catch:** While both models feature adjustable racks and WiFi capability, you actually *have* to use Bosch's smartphone app to access certain features like delay-start and zone washing. Also, if you want interior lighting, a time-remaining indicator, and a built-in water softener, you will have to shell out the extra cash for the Benchmark series.

### 2. The Cleaning Champion: [LG Studio SDNTHS72S](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=LG+Studio+SDNTHS72S+Dishwasher)

* **Overall Score:** 79
* **Estimated Price:** $1,149
* **The Watchdog Take:** According to the lab data, this LG model is the absolute best at washing dishes on this list. It tackles crusted-on food effortlessly and features a specialized wash zone designed specifically for cleaning the inside of bottles. It also scored very well in drying, making it a highly well-rounded machine for the price.

### 3. The Reliability Pick: [Miele G7196SCVISF](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Miele+G7196SCVISF+Dishwasher)

* **Overall Score:** 79
* **Estimated Price:** $2,399
* **The Watchdog Take:** Miele is legendary for its build quality, earning top-tier scores in both predicted reliability and owner satisfaction. This specific model features best-in-class energy efficiency, top-mounted touch controls, and a unique automatic detergent dispenser that perfectly portions your soap for you. However, its drying performance is only middling, which is slightly disappointing for a machine that costs over $2,000.

### 4. The Value Picks: [Bosch 300 Series](https://www.amazon.com/Bosch-240-Volt-Stackable-Condensation-WPA12100UC/dp/B0FBXWQTDR)

* **Models:** Bosch 300 Series SHE53C85N (Score: 81, ~$1,099) | Bosch 300 Series SHE3ADF5N (Score: 79, ~$649)
* **The Watchdog Take:** If you want Bosch's legendary reliability without spending two grand, the 300 Series is where you should look. The SHE53C85N model offers near-top-level washing and noise performance, though it struggles slightly to completely dry plastics. You can also view the [Bosch 300 Series SHE53C85N](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Bosch+300+Series+SHE53C85N+SHE3ADF5N+Dishwasher). If you are on a strict budget, the Lowe's exclusive SHE3ADF5N model drops the price down to $649. It isn't quite as quiet, and the tub uses some plastic instead of being 100% stainless steel, but it still delivers excellent washing performance for the price.

## The Watchdog’s "Avoid" List: The Worst Dishwashers

Just because a dishwasher has a massive price tag or a luxury brand name doesn't mean it will actually clean your dishes. These models completely flopped in CR's lab tests.

### 1. [Viking VDWU524SS](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Viking+VDWU524SS+Dishwasher)

* **Overall Score:** 49
* **Estimated Price:** $2,249
* **The Watchdog Take:** This is the ultimate example of why you shouldn't blindly trust luxury price tags. Despite costing over $2,000, this Viking dishwasher delivered well below-par drying performance. Worse, Viking earns an unfavorable rating for predicted reliability and a rock-bottom, bottom-tier rating for owner satisfaction. Avoid this at all costs.

### 2. [Smeg STU2FABWH2](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Smeg+STU2FABWH2+Dishwasher)

* **Overall Score:** 49
* **Estimated Price:** $1,899
* **The Watchdog Take:** Another overpriced disappointment. For nearly $1,900, you are getting a machine that earned lackluster scores for cleaning dishes on both racks. It is noisy, the drying is strictly average, and your money is better spent literally anywhere else.

### 3. [Frigidaire FDHP4336AS](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Frigidaire+FDHP4336AS+Dishwasher)

* **Overall Score:** 49
* **Estimated Price:** $529 - $810
* **The Watchdog Take:** While this Frigidaire is actually very good at drying, its washing performance is noticeably below average. It is a noisy machine, and according to member surveys, its long-term owner satisfaction score is completely lousy.

### 4. [Hotpoint HDF330PGRWW (Lowe's Exclusive)](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Hotpoint+HDF330PGRWW+Dishwasher)

* **Overall Score:** 48
* **Estimated Price:** $320
* **The Watchdog Take:** This is the lowest-scoring standard dishwasher on the list. Yes, it is incredibly cheap, but you get what you pay for. It features a plastic tub, makes a ton of noise, completely lacks basic features like a third rack or a soil sensor, and struggles to actually get your dishes clean on the upper rack.

---

## The Bottom Line

When it comes to dishwashers, price is not an indicator of performance. You can buy a $649 Bosch that will run reliably for years and clean brilliantly, or you can buy a $2,249 Viking that will leave your plates wet and break down on you. Trust the lab data, prioritize a stainless steel tub if you can, and always check a brand's reliability ratings before you buy.
