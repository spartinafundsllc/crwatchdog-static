When it comes to riding lawn mowers, John Deere absolutely dominates the top of Consumer Reports' charts. But if you look closely at the ratings, there is a massive pricing anomaly that could save you a significant amount of money.

The #1 ranked **John Deere X354** and the #2 ranked **John Deere S240-48** both earn the exact same Overall Score of **84** in Consumer Reports testing. However, their price tags tell a very different story: the X354 costs **$5,100** while the S240-48 costs **$4,000**.

That’s an **$1,100 difference** for two tractors that essentially tie for first place. So, does that extra $1,100 actually get you anything meaningful, or are everyday homeowners just throwing money away on a more expensive model line? Let's break down the data.

### Performance: A Near Dead Heat

In almost every major mowing category, both machines are practically flawless. According to the test results, both the X354 and the S240-48 scored a perfect **5 out of 5** for:
- Side Discharging
- Mulching
- Bagging
- Cutting Evenness

When it comes to actually cutting your grass, you won't notice a difference. They both leave a perfectly manicured lawn.

### Where the X354 Wins (The $1,100 Difference)

So where does the extra money go? 

1. **Handling:** The X354 earns a perfect **5/5 for handling**, while the S240-48 earns a **4/5**. If you have a highly complex yard with lots of tight turns, flower beds, and obstacles, the X354 will be slightly more nimble.
2. **Warranty:** The X354 comes with a generous 4-year warranty, compared to the 3-year warranty on the S240-48.

### Where the S240-48 Actually Wins

Surprisingly, the cheaper S240-48 actually beats the premium X354 in a few key areas:

1. **Deck Size:** The S240-48 features a wider **48-inch deck** compared to the X354's **42-inch deck**. This means you will cover more ground in fewer passes with the cheaper model, reducing your total mowing time.
2. **Noise Levels:** Neither gas tractor is quiet, but the S240-48 scores slightly better for noise at the ear (2/5) compared to the painfully loud X354 (1/5).

### What About the Competition? The Cub Cadet Alternative

If you want the perfect 5/5 handling of the premium X354 but don't want to spend $4,000+, you could look outside the John Deere brand. The **Cub Cadet XT2 SLX50** is a highly recommended alternative.

![Cub Cadet XT2 SLX50](/markdown_images/cub_cadet_xt2.jpg)
*The Cub Cadet XT2 SLX50 ($3,799) features a massive 50-inch deck and 5/5 handling.*

At **$3,799**, it is even cheaper than the John Deere S240-48, yet it boasts:
- A massive **50-inch deck** (wider than both Deeres)
- A perfect **5/5 handling** score
- Perfect **5/5 scores** across all other major performance metrics (side discharging, mulching, bagging, cutting evenness)
- Useful features like electronic fuel injection, a comfortable seat with armrests, LED headlights, and a FastAttach blade system.

However, there is one major catch: reliability. In Consumer Reports' brand member surveys, John Deere is the only brand to earn an above-average rating for predicted reliability and outstanding owner satisfaction. The Cub Cadet, on the other hand, only receives a "just so-so" rating for predicted reliability.

### The Verdict

For the vast majority of homeowners, the **John Deere S240-48 is the smarter buy**. 

Unless your yard is exceptionally hilly or requires extreme precision maneuvering where that 5/5 handling rating is crucial, it is very hard to justify spending an extra $1,100 for the X354. With the S240-48, you get the same top-tier cutting performance, a wider deck that cuts your mowing time, and you get to keep over a thousand dollars in your pocket.
