# These Hybrid Cars Won't Save Gas or Money - Consumer Reports (Raw Draft)

## Overview & Findings
Consumer Reports tested multiple hybrid models against their conventional gas-only counterparts. While hybrid technology is often assumed to generate immediate financial savings, several models carry price premiums so steep or fuel-economy advantages so minor that the financial payback period exceeds typical ownership durations.

### Models Highlighted
1. Subaru Crosstrek Hybrid:
   - Positive powertrain traits, but price premium vs. standard Crosstrek creates an estimated payback period between 6 and 24 years depending on trim and equipment.
2. Honda Accord Hybrid:
   - Redesign cut tested fuel economy from 47 mpg down to 40 mpg.
   - Honda differentiated trims, bundling the hybrid powertrain into higher-cost luxury packages with bigger wheels and wireless CarPlay.
   - Payback period for the most comparable trim is approximately 9 years.
