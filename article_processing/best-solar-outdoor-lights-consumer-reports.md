Good outdoor lighting dramatically extends the hours when you can enjoy your yard. It makes your property safer by highlighting paths, walkways, and stairs, and it serves as beautiful garden accentuation to showcase standout features like a koi pond or slate patio. 

The beauty of solar-powered outdoor lights, in particular, is that there is absolutely no need for complex wiring, trenching, or locating an outdoor outlet. You just mount them or stake them in the ground, let them soak up the sun, and watch your yard light up.

But with hundreds of cheap generic solar lights flooding the market, picking a set that actually lasts through the night can be difficult. 

To help you find the best options, Consumer Reports' test engineers evaluated a wide variety of solar-powered lights from prominent brands under controlled lab conditions. They tested three basic types:
1. **Solar landscape lights** (spotlights or walkway path lights)
2. **Solar deck lights** (stair riser or post-cap lights)
3. **Solar string lights** (decorative accents)

Below are the top-performing models in each category based on CR's rigorous lab tests.

---

## Best Solar Landscape Lights

Landscape lights are designed to illuminate walkways, garden beds, or patio perimeters. Spotlights cast their light in a concentrated beam in a single area, whereas path/walkway lights disperse light evenly in a circular pattern around the fixture.

### [Lerekam Solar Spot Light](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C5TRG9D9?th=1)
* **Overall Score:** 91
* **Estimated Price:** $61.74
* **Lights in Set:** 6
* **Key Strengths:** Outstanding light duration, excellent ease of use

Though Lerekam Solar Spot Lights are on the pricier side, they earned the top spot in CR's tests, boasting a near-perfect Overall Score of **91**. They earned top marks for light duration and ease of use. Each light in the six-pack is equipped with 40 LEDs, producing an intense white light that is perfect for drawing attention to a water feature, stone wall, or ornamental tree. 

These lights offer excellent installation flexibility: you can mount them in loose soil using the included plastic ground spikes, screw them onto flat surfaces (like fences or walls), or angle them as needed. They also feature an on/off button and are rechargeable, meaning they won't let you down during cloudy stretches.

### [Gigalumi Grid Solar Pathway Lights](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B084Z3J1KC)
* **Overall Score:** 73
* **Estimated Price:** $24.99
* **Lights in Set:** 8
* **Key Strengths:** Exceptional budget value, solid light duration

If you want to line a long driveway or garden bed without spending a fortune, the Gigalumi Grid Solar Pathway Lights are hard to beat. Earning a solid Overall Score of **73**, this eight-pack offers some of the lowest per-light costs we've seen. 

Each unit uses a single LED, which casts a dimmer, softer glow than high-powered spotlights. While this is great for creating a subtle nighttime ambience, it may not be bright enough for primary security lighting. The units have a single setting, an on/off button, and a removable battery, but they cannot be charged manually via a cable, nor can they be angled.

## Best Solar Deck Lights

Deck lights add both beauty and safety to outdoor structures, illuminating stairs and railings so that guests can safely navigate elevation changes after dark.

### [JSOT Outdoor Solar Deck Lights](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=JSOT+Outdoor+Solar+Deck+Lights+12+Pack)
* **Overall Score:** 74
* **Estimated Price:** $55.00
* **Lights in Set:** 12
* **Key Strengths:** Top-tier light duration

Designed to be mounted directly to stair risers or other solid wooden surfaces with screws, the JSOT Solar Deck Lights are the only deck lights in CR's test program to earn top marks for light duration. Each of the 12 units in the pack contains 3 LEDs that emit a medium-bright, functional light. 

They are wireless and solar-charged, but they also include a manual on/off switch. If you are expecting a string of overcast winter days, you can turn them off to allow them to build up a full charge. 

### [Deckorators 4x4 Solar Outdoor Post Cap Light](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Deckorators+4x4+Solar+Outdoor+Post+Cap+Light)
* **Overall Score:** 64
* **Estimated Price:** $11.98 - $18.99
* **Lights in Set:** 1
* **Key Strengths:** Good light duration, clean aesthetic

If you want to dress up your deck railings, the Deckorators 4x4 Solar Outdoor Post Cap is the top-scoring model in CR's post-cap testing. Equipped with a single LED, it casts light outward and downward from all four sides of the fixture, making it excellent for highlighting deck boundaries. 

It is designed to rest on standard 4x4 wooden posts and mounts using adhesive rather than screws. It comes with a removable battery and is available in four finishes (black, white, brown, and gray) to match your deck's color scheme. Note that it lacks an on/off switch and cannot be charged manually.

## Best Solar String Lights

String lights are the perfect way to add a festive, warm, and inviting flair to a backyard pergola, arbor, or patio dining area.

### [Brightech Pro Solar Powered Outdoor String Lights](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B075NS8YXG)
* **Overall Score:** 78
* **Estimated Price:** $21.99
* **String Length:** 27 feet (12 bulbs)
* **Key Strengths:** Bright Edison-style bulbs, top-tier ease of use

The Brightech Pro Solar String Lights earned an Overall Score of **78**, topping the solar string light category. They feature 12 Edison-style oval-shaped bulbs spaced 20 inches apart along a 27-foot cord. Each bulb is packed with 24 tiny LEDs, generating an exceptionally bright, warm glow.

The long cord provides excellent design flexibility; you can hang the strand linearly for subtle lighting, or double it up to concentrate brightness over a dining table. They feature replaceable batteries and an on/off button. While CR's extended exposure testing noted that three of the bulbs filled with water over time, none of them stopped working, proving their impressive outdoor resilience.
