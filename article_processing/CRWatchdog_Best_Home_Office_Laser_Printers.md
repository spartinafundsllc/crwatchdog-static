Let's be honest: owning an inkjet printer is a miserable experience. If you aren't printing every single day, the liquid ink dries up, the nozzles clog, and a set of replacement cartridges can cost as much as the printer itself. (For a deep dive into avoiding printer frustration, check out our guide to the [Best Printers for People Who Hate Printers](/best-printers-for-people-who-hate-printers/).)

If you are setting up a home office, do yourself a favor and make the switch to a laser printer. According to the latest lab testing from [Consumer Reports](https://www.consumerreports.org/), laser printers completely outperform inkjets when it comes to text quality, print speed, and most importantly, long-term reliability. Because they use dry toner powder, they never dry out, and they are significantly cheaper to operate over time.

While they do have a larger footprint and struggle with high-resolution photo printing, they are the undisputed kings of the home office. Here are the top CR-Recommended laser printers, broken down by category. 

---

## 1. Best Compact Laser Printer
### [Canon imageCLASS LBP247dw](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Canon+imageCLASS+LBP247dw)
*   **Overall Score:** 82
*   **Estimated Price:** $364.00
*   **Estimated Toner Cost:** $12/year
*   **The Watchdog Take:** If you just need a straightforward machine to churn out black-and-white documents, contracts, and shipping labels, this is your winner. It earns top marks across the board for text speed and text quality. It features auto-duplexing (two-sided printing) and is incredibly easy to use. 
*   **The Alternative:** If you want a higher paper capacity, the **Brother HL-L6310DW** is a solid alternative. It performs nearly as well, and CR surveys show that Brother single-function monochrome printers actually pull slightly higher owner satisfaction scores than Canon.

## 2. Best Multifunction B&W Laser Printer
### [Brother MFC-L5915DW](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Brother+MFC-L5915DW)
*   **Overall Score:** 81
*   **Estimated Price:** $589.99 - $666.35
*   **Estimated Toner Cost:** $11/year
*   **The Watchdog Take:** Most people do 99% of their printing in black and white. If you don't mind going to an office supply store for that rare color print, this monochrome all-in-one is an absolute powerhouse. It gets top marks for speed and excellent text quality. Best of all, it gives you a built-in scanner, copier, and a highly convenient auto-document feeder. Brother's all-in-one monochrome machines also boast excellent marks for both predicted reliability and owner satisfaction, making this a safe bet for a long-term investment.

## 3. Best Single-Function Color Laser Printer
### [Brother HL-L3295CDW](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Brother+HL-L3295CDW)
*   **Overall Score:** 79
*   **Estimated Price:** $429.99 - $481.98
*   **Estimated Toner Cost:** $89/year
*   **The Watchdog Take:** Color laser technology has historically been incredibly expensive, but this compact Brother unit brings it down to a highly accessible price point. The text quality is exemplary, and it pulls "very good" ratings for both speed and graphics. We love the practical design: it features a 250-sheet main tray and a 30-sheet secondary input tray, and—crucially—it allows you to keep printing in black and white even if the color toner runs dry. 

## 4. Best Do-It-All Color Laser Printer
### [Brother MFC-L3765CDW](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Brother+MFC-L3765CDW)
*   **Overall Score:** 72
*   **Estimated Price:** $390.00
*   **Estimated Toner Cost:** $96/year
*   **The Watchdog Take:** This is the Swiss Army knife of home office printers. It does it all: printing, copying, scanning, and faxing, all in full color. For under $400, it is on the lower end of the pricing spectrum for color lasers, but it still managed to earn CR's highest rating for text quality. Add in an automatic document feeder and auto-duplexing capabilities, and you have a versatile workhorse that will handle anything your business throws at it. 

---

## The Bottom Line

When buying a printer, don't just look at the price tag on the box—look at the running costs. An inkjet printer might seem like a bargain at $80, but it can easily saddle you with $70 to $100 in ink costs every single year. A monochrome laser printer like the **Canon imageCLASS** or **Brother MFC-L5915DW** will only cost you about $11 or $12 a year in toner, quickly paying for itself while delivering vastly superior reliability.
